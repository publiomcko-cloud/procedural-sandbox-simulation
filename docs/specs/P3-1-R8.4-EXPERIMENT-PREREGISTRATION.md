## Root oracle frozen before candidate execution

For each fixed X− ray and each of triangle 3103's source edges:

1. Evaluate `Transition.sample_canonical` on the exact frozen FILL/CARVE snapshot at 4,096 uniformly spaced intervals (4,097 endpoints) across the frozen finite segment. Record every exact-zero sample and every adjacent sign-changing bracket; do not collapse multiple brackets into a single root.
2. Bisect each sign-changing bracket for at most 48 iterations or until scalar midpoint stagnation or adjacent `Vector3` evaluations become identical at the pinned Godot precision. Record endpoint vector bits, endpoint densities, final interval width, root position/density, and the active base/ordered-operation branch at endpoints, midpoint, and root. Do not claim binary64 positional resolution: this Godot build uses the engine's `Vector3` component precision, which the evidence must identify.
3. Record discrete local extrema of `abs(density)` as *possible tangent-root candidates* with their bracket and values. These are uncertainty observations, not certified roots.
4. Report roots found by sign changes separately from possible tangencies. A finite scan cannot certify root completeness or exclude roots narrower than its spacing, even-multiplicity roots, or a root at a branch corner that does not change sign.

Surface displacement is Euclidean distance from frozen mesh/ray intersections to the closest independently bracketed continuous root on the same frozen ray/edge. Report unmatched roots and root-count discrepancies separately; do not claim global Hausdorff bounds. Root positional uncertainty is at least the final bracket width, with additional binary32 source-sample and canonical evaluator precision disclosed. No numerical geometry acceptance threshold is introduced.

## Candidate algorithm and stop-loss accounting

One candidate only: **canonical cell refinement closure**. Begin with the failing LOD1-side cell made from eight LOD0 cells. The native neighboring LOD0 chunk is the half-open world box `[16,20) × [-2,8) × [6,9)`; relative to the transition-cell origin `(15,2,7)`, its unit-cell indices satisfy `x∈[1,5)`, `y∈[-4,6)`, `z∈[-1,2)`. Cells in that native box are comparison neighbors, never propagation candidates. Extract fine/coarse cells using `Lod01TransitionFixture.build_uniform_reference_cell` and the unchanged `MarchingTetrahedra` tetrahedron/cross-squared cutoff (`<= 1e-12` omitted). For every fine-to-coarse/native face, compare actual extracted world-space contour segment sets using the R8.2 triangle-plane extraction and `1e-5` integer point key. Promote a LOD1-side coarse cell to fine only when its actual shared contour set mismatches; repeat in deterministic face order. This is a propagation feasibility experiment, not a new transition mesher. If the closure does not terminate with zero mismatched active interfaces within the limits, the minimal conforming gate fails and all later candidate gates remain unrun.

Prospective research stop-loss (not product budgets): at most four face-neighbor propagation rings from the seeded cell; at most 10,000 unique canonical sample lattice nodes in the inspected ROI; at most 20,000 simultaneously emitted ROI triangles; and at most 600,000,000 microseconds of focused candidate wall time per candidate. Count unique half-unit lattice coordinates and actual retained mesh triangles; report cache/temporary builds separately. Check limits before each promotion/build. Never lower the unchanged degeneracy cutoff, alter field/ledger math, or relax topology checks to obtain a pass.

Mandatory advance invariants: zero unexplained internal open edges, zero unmatched shared-interface contours, zero nonmanifold geometry, zero emitted triangles at/below the unchanged degeneracy threshold, and no unintended winding mismatch against the canonical outward gradient. A crop edge is classified separately and cannot be misreported as an internal edge. No geometric acceptance tolerance is approved.

## Sequential execution gates

1. Verify source, executable, manifest, fixture and prior evidence hashes.
2. Run canonical input/source preflight and frozen root oracle; preserve detected roots and uncertainty.
3. Reproduce the R8.2 X+ transition mismatch and controls.
4. Run exactly one bounded canonical-refinement-closure candidate for the minimal X+ fixture. If mandatory topology or resource gate fails, record the failure and stop candidate execution.
5. Only after gate 4 passes: run the mirrored and same-LOD interface controls.
6. Only after controls pass: run X− root-aware extraction, triangle 3103, then ordered FILL/CARVE.
7. Only after all frozen gates pass: perform bounded comparisons with uniform 0.25/0.125 references.
8. Run focused diagnostics and full `scripts/validate.ps1` against final test-only source. Full regression PASS does not imply geometry PASS.

There are no retries of a failed frozen candidate gate. The final report must mark every unexecuted stage `NOT_RUN_AFTER_PREDECESSOR_GATE`, preserve all raw attempts, and keep performance and geometry acceptance unscored.
