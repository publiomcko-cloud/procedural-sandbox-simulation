## Recommended next experiment

Do not promote any candidate. If another bounded experiment is authorized, prioritize A's shared-face/topology ownership and the 9/25 ray mismatch, with a complete neighboring-chunk contour oracle. For C, first isolate the three newly reported neighbor nonmanifold edges, then decide whether any smoothing arm can be admitted to a new frozen contract. For D, retain the separate volume/extractor distinction, but debug Candidate D's triangle assembly and X+ boundary before considering storage. Any further comparison should split materialization, each extractor, ray reference, and cache work into separate timed phases. No product geometry/performance tolerances were approved by these experiments.
