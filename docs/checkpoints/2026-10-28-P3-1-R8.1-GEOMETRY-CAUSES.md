## Findings and limits

1. **Cell 712 / triangle 3103:** triangle bytes are unchanged by the R7.3 repair. Its winding follows the empty side of the sampled tetrahedral linear field, but opposes the stable continuous canonical edited-field outward gradient at the centroid. This isolates a local piecewise-linear versus continuous-field disagreement, not a global vertex-order defect. The exact local sampling/CSG/interpolation cause remains UNKNOWN. Reported cell-pair normal angles come from the R7 fixture's per-triangle flat normals, not production's density-gradient shared vertex normals.
2. **X+ negative-side LOD1 seam:** 33 versus 35 extracted boundary segments and two unmatched segments are confirmed by both the existing contour check and independent raw triangle-edge incidence. Shared face densities match bitwise at 533 samples; opposite LOD orientation passes. Exact face decomposition/ownership/interpolation branch remains UNKNOWN.
3. **X− picking offset:** the picker agrees with the independent continuous-field root within about `1.79e-5`, while the first same-ray union mesh hit is about `0.36609` world units farther away and has positive canonical density. This is a mesh-versus-continuous-field discrepancy, not evidence of a picker transform/first-hit error. Exact mesher branch remains UNKNOWN.
