## Findings

1. **Triangle 3103 / cell 712:** independent reconstruction from the original tetrahedron and binary32 samples reproduces the emitted triangle. Its winding is consistent with the sampled piecewise-linear tetrahedron, while the continuous ordered CSG SDF gradient at the centroid points in the opposite direction (dot `-0.468217521905899`). Continuous edge roots differ from linear interpolation by `0.00957–0.01438` world units, with CSG branch changes on two edges. This frozen case demonstrates a continuous-field versus sampled piecewise-linear reconstruction discrepancy, not a coordinate transform or general winding defect.
2. **X+ interface at `x=16`:** all 533 shared face density probes match bitwise, but negative-side LOD1 emits 33 segments against 35 on the positive-side LOD0/LOD1 references. The explicit-coordinate face oracle independently reconstructs two missing contour segments. The transition fan triangles that would carry these segments have squared cross magnitudes `9.47780916249474e-13` and `9.34902979685126e-13`, below the unchanged `1e-12` omission cutoff; corresponding neighboring facets are just above it. This is a real transition-mesh interface asymmetry, not a scalar, ownership or contour-oracle defect.
3. **X− ray / mesh:** the picker is within about `1.79e-5` of the continuous SDF root, while adjacent LOD0 mesh representations hit about `0.36611` world units farther along the same ray, on positive canonical density. Recomputed source samples and chunk meshes match exactly. This identifies coarse spatial sampling and edge-linear interpolation of the nonlinear continuous field; the X+ LOD0/LOD0 ray control agrees within `1.3e-7`.

No prototype correction was attempted because the R8.2 scope was causal provenance; the X+ source would require a separately bounded geometry repair decision that preserves or explicitly replaces the degeneracy contract. No new tolerance was adopted.
