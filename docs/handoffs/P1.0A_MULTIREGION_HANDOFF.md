Implementation summary: canonical region cells are half-open X/Z `[0,16) × [0,12)` at 0.5-unit spacing, with mathematical floor for negative coordinates. Each region owns 32×24 cells in a 4×4 grid of 8×6-cell chunks. The fixed preview instantiates 9 regions and 144 chunk meshes centered on `(0,0)`. SDF samples and extraction use canonical world-space positions before mesh vertices are converted to chunk-local coordinates. The C visualization remains one global plane at world Z=6. Brush edits are accepted only in the safe interior of the center region; neighboring regions and boundary-crossing brush spheres are rejected without mutation. Picker work is bounded at 1,200 steps to cover the allowed 96-unit camera zoom. Runtime diagnostics report elapsed mesh-build time, triangles and a lower-bound estimate of packed position/normal bytes.

Architectural impact: world seed, generator version, region keys and ordered edits remain data in `TerrainWorldState`, separate from scene nodes. This preserves a basis for future multiplayer replication without adding networking. No streaming, persistence or cross-region editing is included.

Dependencies added / licenses: none; Godot 4.7.2 and GDScript only.
