## Finding

Both accepted job logs show successful compilation and final linker commands for the Windows x86_64 editor and `template_release` extension DLLs. The commands list SQLite 3.51.0, LZ4 1.10.0, sixteen meshoptimizer v0.25 objects, three FastNoiseLite 1.1.1 objects, and the target-specific godot-cpp static archive. The logs resolve godot-cpp to commit `9c8aeff0f58ad030f3d1030e8262de1322cd0ccd`.

These are publisher-recorded **linker inputs**. They do not establish what machine-code sections remained after optimization, all runtime imports or transitive dependencies, or complete DLL composition. They are enough to make the listed licenses and their notice-retention conditions material to a future redistribution review. They do not establish a violation or compliance.

The accepted F1 package inventory reports the Voxel Tools project `LICENSE.md`, but no separate LZ4, meshoptimizer, FastNoiseLite, or godot-cpp notices. The proposed full text bundle is only a research artifact in this repository. It was not added to the vendor package and is not evidence that the original package included those notices.

## Notice draft and future package treatment

The companion [draft notice bundle](../legal/P0.1-F1.6-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES-DRAFT.md) includes complete text for LZ4, meshoptimizer, FastNoiseLite, and godot-cpp as found in the pinned Voxel Tools source, and separately reproduces the existing Voxel Tools MIT notice. SQLite, FastNoise2, and Tracy are documented without inventing or asserting a required notice.

For a future distribution only, a separately reviewed `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` file alongside the plugin/distribution license materials is a practical placement candidate. It should carry the full required texts for every component determined to be included and the existing Voxel Tools project notice. Before use, compare the future package's exact binaries and dependency inventory with this historical evidence and obtain owner authorization and legal review. No production package or vendor notice was changed in F1.6.

## Owner decision and recommended next steps

1. **Distribution gate — hold.** Do not make a distribution or compliance claim from this draft. The notice texts are traceable, but full binary contents, dependencies, intended redistribution terms, and legal sufficiency remain unresolved.
2. **Upstream provenance/notice inquiry — recommended next evidence step.** The unsent draft below asks Voxel Tools maintainers for intended notices, per-DLL component/SBOM or link-map data, runtime dependency inventory, and clarification of anything beyond logged inputs. Sending it requires separate owner authorization. A response would inform, not by itself certify, the package.
3. **Future corrected redistribution package — only after authorization and legal review.** If redistribution remains in scope, obtain a sufficiently complete dependency/provenance inventory, resolve all notices and distribution terms, and prepare a corrected package under a separate authorization. The current bundle is not that package.
4. **Possible isolated F2 experiment — separate decision.** A later strictly isolated, internal, nonredistributed Godot compatibility experiment could answer runtime questions that these documents cannot. It requires separate owner authorization, a documented security/runtime isolation plan, and an explicit acceptable legal-risk decision. This task did not authorize F2, extension installation, binary execution, or dependency adoption. Internal evaluation and commercial distribution pose different questions; neither is cleared here.
