# Benchmark protocol/result
Candidate SHA; Godot version; renderer; addon revision and license; OS; CPU; GPU; RAM; viewport resolution; seed and generator version; voxel size; chunk size; view distance; mods enabled; metric tool; warmup and run length.

Report median, p95 and p99 frame times, mean/peak RAM, chunk generation timing, brush-to-visible/collision latency, stale update count, serialization throughput, replay/checkpoint accuracy. At least three runs where possible; note variance and failures. Do not present targets as measured achievements.
